Quadrotor differential flatness (Q3)

Trajectory:
  x = 10 cos(t) / (1 + sin^2(t))
  y = 10 sin(t) cos(t) / (1 + sin^2(t))
  z = 10

Velocity and acceleration (analytic):
  vx = -10 sin(t) (3 - sin^2(t)) / (1 + sin^2(t))^2
  vy = 10 (1 - 3 sin^2(t)) / (1 + sin^2(t))^2
  ax = (fp * d - 4 f sin(t) cos(t)) / d^3, fp = -30 cos^3(t), f = -10 sin(t) (3 - sin^2(t))
  ay = (gp * d - 4 g sin(t) cos(t)) / d^3, gp = -60 sin(t) cos(t), g = 10 (1 - 3 sin^2(t))

Attitude construction (FLU):
  psi = atan2(vy, vx)
  b3 = (a + g e3) / ||a + g e3||
  b1d = [cos(psi), sin(psi), 0]^T
  b2 = (b3 x b1d) / ||b3 x b1d||
  b1 = b2 x b3
  R = [b1 b2 b3], q = quat(R), normalized with w >= 0
